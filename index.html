WHAT YOU GET

Three layers of protection.

LIVE HAZARD INTEL

Real-time data from NASA FIRMS, USGS earthquakes, NOAA severe weather, and public utility outages — fused into one location-aware risk score updating around you.

INSTANT SOS

One tap shares your live GPS, current risk conditions, and a timestamp with up to five trusted contacts — over SMS, no app required on their end.

CRASH DETECTION

On-device motion sensors detect potential vehicle impacts. A 30-second cancellable countdown gives you control. We never auto-dial 911.

HOW IT WORKS

From signal to safety
in under a second.

01

WE LISTEN

Public hazard feeds from NASA, USGS, NOAA, and utility providers stream into our analysis service every minute.

02

WE FUSE

Your location triggers a query that combines fire, earthquake, weather, and infrastructure signals into a single 0–100 risk score.

03

YOU DECIDE

If something happens, you tap SOS. We share your GPS and conditions with your contacts. You control everything.

04

NO MIDDLEMAN

Your contacts receive a standard SMS. No app to install. No account. Just a link to your live location and the conditions around you.

ORIGIN

Built by someone who needed it.

Hazard Intelligence was started by a New Orleans-based founder who lived through Katrina. The platform exists because most "safety" apps are either insurance products dressed up as features or social networks pretending to care. This is neither. It's a single, honest tool for one job: helping you and the people you love know what's happening and act on it.
